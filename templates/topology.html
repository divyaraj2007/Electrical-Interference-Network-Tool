Physical & Logical Network Topology

Visualize the end-to-end path and leverage multi-target hop telemetry to isolate whether signal degradation is constrained to the local Ethernet drop or exists in the upstream infrastructure.

End-to-End Segment Telemetry Map
Sender PC → Ethernet Switch → Router / Gateway → Upstream Internet
Hop 1: Physical Cable Drop

Connects Sender PC to the Ethernet Switch. If EMI from motors or bad crimps exist, frame corruptions (CRC/FCS) and packet drops occur directly at this link.

Hop 2: Default Gateway

Measures router internal interface latency. Normal values are under 1.5 ms. Increases here indicate local queueing delays or switch fabric congestion.

Hop 3: WAN & DNS Resolution

Differentiates Internet / ISP anomalies. If Hop 1 & 2 are healthy but Hop 3 exhibits loss, the problem is external upstream routing, not local EMI.
